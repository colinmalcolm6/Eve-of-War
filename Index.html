How to play

The goal

  • Eliminate every rival, form an alliance that outlasts them all, or hold 75% of the world's income.

Clock & disasters

  • Each turn lasts 60 seconds, plus 10 seconds for every territory you capture. When time runs out the turn ends automatically. Watch the clock at the top right of the map.
  • Rarely, nature strikes where it really does: hurricanes and typhoons on tropical coasts and seas, tornadoes on the plains, wildfires in dry forest country, earthquakes on fault lines, floods on river lowlands, blizzards in the far north. Armies (and sometimes fighters or fleets) are lost.
  • Fleets fire from their own zone and stay put, so they can fight again and again each turn (each shot costs one order, like air strikes).

Setup

  • Players take turns placing one army on one unclaimed territory until the world is claimed, then each adds five reinforcements, one placement per turn.

Your turn

  • Income: each territory pays by its GDP — industrial heartlands pay far more than colonies. Spend it on armies ($3), fighters ($8), fleets ($10) with Build.
  • Attack: select your territory, then an adjacent enemy. Risk dice: up to 3 vs 2, ties go to the defender. Fighters in a territory add +1 to its best dice.
  • Sea: fleets sit in sea zones. Build one where you own a coast. Fleets fight fleets, bombard enemy coasts (5–6 hits), and let your armies invade across water from a shared zone.
  • Air: fighters strike up to two territories away, killing defenders on 4+ (never the last army) while defending fighters shoot back on 5+.
  • Conquering a territory also captures any fighters stationed there. Dashed gold lines link the Soviet Far East ⇄ Alaska and Brazil ⇄ West Africa.
  • Moving fleets, fortifying, bombing and air strikes cost 1 of your 4 orders per turn. Battles are free.

Diplomacy

  • Propose alliances under Powers — allies cannot attack each other. Break one any time. Use Taunts to rattle your rivals.

Bots & music

  • Solo play: pick Play solo vs bots in the lobby. In a shared room the host can add up to 7 bots beside friends.
  • Add your own original music under ♪ Music; it stays on your device.

Borders are hand-drawn approximations of the 1939 political map; GDP figures are rough estimates.
